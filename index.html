About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Astronomical Institute of the Czech Academy of Sciences in Prague, where I work as part of the ALMA-JELLY Collaboration.

My research focuses on understanding the physical processes regulating star formation across various galactic environments, spanning internal stellar feedback in isolated galaxies to environmental drivers like ram pressure stripping in nearby galaxy clusters.

Projects

Analysis of ALMA JELLY galaxies

ALMA JELLY is a Large Program (2021.1.01616.L; PI: Jáchym, coPI: Ming Sun, Masafumi Yagi) to investigate the cold molecular gas contents traced by carbon monoxide (CO 2--1) line transition with ALMA Band 6 observations in jellyfish galaxies in nearby galaxy clusters Coma, Leo, and Norma. By focussing on one of these galaxies, ESO 137-001 in the Norma Cluster, I probe atomic and molecular gas under different excitation conditions and understand its potential transformation into stars.
